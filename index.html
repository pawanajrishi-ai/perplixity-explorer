How perplexity works

A language model gives each token a probability based on the tokens before it. Perplexity is the geometric mean of the inverse probabilities.

PPL = ( 1/P₁ × 1/P₂ × … × 1/PN )1/N

The same value is the exponential of the cross-entropy loss, the average of −ln P.

PPL = exp( −(1/N) Σ ln Pᵢ )

A perplexity of 10 means the model was as unsure as if it had to pick among 10 equally likely tokens at each step.

1 is certainty and lower is better. Only compare scores from the same model and tokenizer on comparable text.
